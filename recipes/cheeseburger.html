Cheeseburger Recipe

Description

This is a cheeseburger recipe. Get the bread, lettuce, tomato, pickles, cheddar and beef. Cooke the beef, put the burger together. Eat!

Ingredients

  • Beef
  • Cheese
  • Lettuce
  • Tomatoe
  • Pickle
  • Onion
  • Bun
  • Condiments of choice

Steps

  1. Prepare beef in preferred manner. While cooking move forward to other steps.
  2. Get cheese slices.
  3. Wash and chop lettuce.
  4. Wash and chop tomatoe.
  5. Ready the pickle.
  6. Chop onion.
  7. Toast bun.
  8. Pull condiments of choice.
  9. Once meat is prepared, order ingredients and eat!
