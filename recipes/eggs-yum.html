Eggs-yum Recipe

Description

This is an eggs-yum recipe. It's simple and real good.

Ingredients

  • Eggs: 3
  • Cheese
  • 2%/FF Milk: 1/4 cup
  • Oil
  • Onion
  • Sweet peppers
  • Salt
  • Hot sauce
  • Potatoes

Steps

  1. Chop onions, peppers and potatoes into bit sizes bits.
  2. Throw chopped ingredients and oil into pan. Turn on to medium heat.
  3. Crack and beat eggs into small bowl, add milk, set aside.
  4. Move pan ingredients about until onions and translucent and potatoes are cooked.
  5. Pour egg mixture into pan, add salt to taste.
  6. Cook stirring until eggs are ready, only a few minutes.
  7. Eat! With hot sauce of choice, of course.
